# Notes: Night Shift visual polish

## Brand direction

- Territory: **Controlled Emergency** — calm operational control during a cold-chain incident.
- Primary proof remains the real command UI, receipts, manifest verification, and IAM-denial contrast.
- Generated assets must never contain text, fake product UI, people, robots, laboratory stock scenes, or generic AI decoration.
- Existing PRD visual constraints: white canvas, hairline borders, compact density, monochrome typography, electric blue as the active accent, 12px cards, 8px buttons, and no generic gradient-heavy design.

## Asset decision

- The transparent paper-and-trace composition is used as an atmospheric proof artifact on the public landing hero.
